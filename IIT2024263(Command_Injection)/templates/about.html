About Harborline

Harborline started in 2014 when two systems administrators got tired of watching small businesses get passed between overseas support queues every time a server went down. We set out to run infrastructure the way we'd want it run for our own projects: fewer accounts to juggle, fewer surprise charges, and a support team that actually knows the servers they're talking about.

Today we manage hosting for a few hundred small businesses — mostly agencies, local retailers, and independent consultants who'd rather spend their time on their own work than on server maintenance.

Where we are

Our support team and primary data center partner are both based in the same metro area, which keeps latency low for most of our customers and means someone is usually a short drive from the hardware.
